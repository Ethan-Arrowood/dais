Why keep dragons at all?

  • Perimeter defense — a single wyrm replaces forty archers
  • Metallurgy — forge-grade flame on demand
  • Diplomacy — negotiations conclude noticeably faster
  • Companionship — loyal for six centuries, give or take
The archers statistic is from the Battle of Thornfield. Cite it.

Know your charge

Temperament by breed

  • Emberback — placid, food-motivated
  • Frostwing — aloof, respects routine
  • Stormcrest — do not keep a Stormcrest

Daily provisions

Two oxen, one cartload of coal, and — this is not optional — conversation. A bored dragon redecorates.

Housing: the castle question

Retrofitting your keep

  1. Widen the gatehouse to at least nine spans
  2. Fireproof the great hall (stone, slate, zero tapestries)
  3. Convert the tallest tower to a roost — dragons sleep high
  4. Warn the village. Repeatedly. In writing.
The tapestry point is where you tell the story about Lord Bramwell.

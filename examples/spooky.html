Haunting fundamentals

  • Location — attics and cellars outperform kitchens 3-to-1
  • Timing — 3:07 a.m. is the industry standard
  • Restraint — one cold draft beats ten rattling chains
  • Consistency — haunt the same stair. Brand recognition.
The kitchen statistic is because people just assume it's the fridge.

The modern specter's toolkit

Audio

Floorboard creaks, distant music boxes, whispered names (first names only).

Visual

Peripheral movement, flickering lamps, fog with no obvious source.

Thermal

The classic cold spot. Reliable, low-effort, extremely reviewable.

Seasonal operations

October: peak season protocol

  1. Coordinate with local pumpkins — carved allies hold the porch
  2. Yield the streets to costumed children; they outrank you tonight
  3. Reserve the good manifestations for skeptics
  4. November 1st: back to subtle. Have some dignity.
The "outrank you" line lands better if delivered deadpan.
